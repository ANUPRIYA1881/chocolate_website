01 — Origin

Grown in
the shade.

See how it is made

Every bar starts on a shaded farm, where the pods ripen from green to amber and red.

We buy straight from three family-run estates and pay above the fair-trade floor, so the people who grow the cacao share in what it becomes.

Harvest
Cut by hand, pod by pod.
Ferment
Six days under banana leaf.
Dry
Raised beds in open sun.
02 — Craft

Five slow steps,
no shortcuts.

Roast
  1. Step 1

    Roast

    Small drums, low heat, until the beans smell of toasted nuts and dried fruit.

  2. Step 2

    Grind

    Granite stones crush the nibs into a warm, glossy liquor.

  3. Step 3

    Conche

    Seventy-two hours of slow folding smooths every rough edge.

  4. Step 4

    Temper

    Cooled and warmed by hand until the cocoa butter sets with a clean snap.

  5. Step 5

    Rest

    A week in the dark, while the cocoa butter settles into a fine crystal bloom.

04 — The snap

Listen for
the snap.

A well-tempered bar breaks clean and rings like glass. It is the first thing we check on every batch, and the last thing we check before it leaves the atelier.
